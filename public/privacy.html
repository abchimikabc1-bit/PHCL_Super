Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how PHCL Super collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our platform.

1. Data Collection

We collect only the data necessary for account creation, KYC verification, transaction processing, and improving user experience. This includes name, email, phone number, biometric liveness data, and wallet addresses.

2. Data Usage

All data is encrypted at rest using AES‑256‑GCM and transmitted over TLS 1.3. Data is used solely for identity verification, escrow management, and providing the services you request.

3. Data Sharing

We never sell your personal data. We may share anonymised analytics with trusted partners but no personally identifiable information is disclosed.

4. Your Rights

You can request data export, correction, or deletion by contacting support. Full deletion removes all your data from our servers within 30 days.

5. Cookies

We use essential cookies for session management and optional analytics cookies that you can opt‑out of via the settings panel.

For any questions, please contact privacy@phclsuper.com.
